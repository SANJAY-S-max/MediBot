COMMUNITY HEALTH AND DISEASE AWARENESS INFORMATION

COMMON INFECTIOUS DISEASES:
1. COMMON COLD:
   - Cause: Rhinovirus, spread through nasal secretions.
   - Prevention: Avoid rubbing eyes or nose, clean shared surfaces, practice respiratory etiquette.
   - Diet: Warm soups, herbal teas, vitamin C rich fruits.
   
2. INFLUENZA (FLU):
   - Cause: Influenza viruses type A or B.
   - Danger signs: Rapid breathing, persistent high fever, chest pain.
   - Prevention: Annual vaccination, isolation of active cases.
   
3. MALARIA & DENGUE (MOSQUITO-BORNE):
   - Cause: Mosquito bites (Anopheles for Malaria, Aedes for Dengue).
   - Prevention: Eliminate standing water pools, spray larvicides, use bed nets and mosquito repellants containing DEET.
   - Action: Check blood counts if high fever is accompanied by joint pains or skin rashes.

CHRONIC LIFESTYLE DISORDERS:
1. GASTROESOPHAGEAL REFLUX (GERD / ACIDITY):
   - Cause: Backflow of gastric acid irritating the esophageal lining.
   - Prevention: Avoid carbonated drinks, caffeine, oily/fried foods, and hot spices.
   - Lifestyle: Avoid heavy dinners. Keep a gap of 2-3 hours between meals and sleep. Raise the bed head by 6 inches.
   
2. ESSENTIAL HYPERTENSION:
   - Danger signs: Severe headache, chest pain, blurry vision, nosebleeds.
   - Prevention: Regular blood pressure tracking, reducing stress through meditation, and low-sodium diets.
   
3. CHRONIC BRONCHITIS / COPD:
   - Cause: Cigarette smoke, air pollution, biomass fuel emissions in unventilated kitchens.
   - Lifestyle: Stop smoking, avoid dusty environments, use clean cookstoves, do breathing exercises.
