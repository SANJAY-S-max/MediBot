WHO CLINICAL GUIDELINES & PROTOCOLS

CARDIOVASCULAR EMERGENCIES:
- Chest pain is a key warning sign of Myocardial Infarction (heart attack).
- Acute myocardial infarction symptoms include squeezing, fullness, pressure, or crushing pain in the center of the chest. Pain may radiate to the left arm, neck, jaw, back, or stomach.
- Accompanying symptoms: shortness of breath (dyspnea), cold sweats, nausea, lightheadedness, or dizziness.
- Immediately seek professional medical attention if symptoms last more than 10 minutes or worsen.

STROKE DETECTION (FAST PROTOCOL):
- Face Drooping: One side of the face droops or is numb. Ask the person to smile.
- Arm Weakness: One arm feels weak or numb. Ask the person to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?
- Speech Difficulty: Speech is slurred, or the person is unable to speak or hard to understand.
- Time to Call Emergency Services: If the person shows any of these symptoms, even if the symptoms go away, check the time so you can tell the medical team when they began, and transport immediately to a stroke-ready hospital.

ACUTE RESPIRATORY INFECTIONS:
- Severe shortness of breath, rapid shallow breathing, cyanosis (bluish lips or skin), and confusion are critical indicators of hypoxia.
- Influenza and COVID-19 transmission occurs via respiratory droplets. Key preventions are mask wearing in crowded spaces, annual vaccinations, and frequent hand hygiene with soap or alcohol rub.
- Pneumonia causes alveolar inflammation. Symptoms include fever, chills, productive cough with colored sputum, and chest pain during deep inhalation.

INFECTIOUS DISEASE MANAGEMENT:
- Malaria is a parasitic infection transmitted by the bite of female Anopheles mosquitoes. Classic symptoms include cyclic high fever, shivering chills, sweating, and headache. Diagnosed via blood smear. Prevention: insecticide-treated nets, vector control, chemical prophylaxis.
- Typhoid fever is a life-threatening systemic infection caused by Salmonella enterica serovar Typhi. Transmitted via contaminated food or water. Symptoms: sustained high fever, fatigue, headache, abdominal pain, diarrhea or constipation. Safe food and clean water are primary preventions.
- Cholera is an acute diarrheal infection caused by ingestion of food or water contaminated with Vibrio cholerae. Causes severe watery diarrhea and rapid dehydration. Main therapy is Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) and IV fluids.
