GOVERNMENT MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE GUIDELINES

DIABETES MELLITUS MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL:
- Type 2 Diabetes is a chronic metabolic condition characterized by insulin resistance and hyperglycemia.
- Screening parameters: Fasting blood glucose >= 126 mg/dL, or Random blood glucose >= 200 mg/dL, or HbA1c >= 6.5%.
- First-line lifestyle changes: Reduction of refined carbohydrates, increasing dietary fiber, performing 150 minutes of moderate physical activity weekly.
- Medication adherence is critical. Regular diabetic monitoring includes biannual HbA1c testing, annual fundus eye exams to check for retinopathy, and urine tests for microalbuminuria.

HYPERTENSION AND CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH:
- Hypertension is defined as systolic blood pressure >= 140 mmHg and/or diastolic blood pressure >= 90 mmHg.
- Dietary sodium restriction: Limit salt intake to less than 5 grams per day (equivalent to one teaspoon).
- Encourage diets rich in potassium (vegetables, bananas, beans) unless contraindicated by chronic kidney disease.
- Cessation of tobacco products and restriction of alcohol consumption. Regular blood pressure checkups are recommended at all primary health centers.

MATERNAL AND CHILD HEALTH GUIDELINES:
- Pregnant women should receive at least 4 antenatal checkups.
- Supplementation: Daily iron and folic acid (IFA) supplements starting from the second trimester for 180 days to prevent anemia.
- Mandatory immunizations: Tetanus toxoid injection for mothers, BCG, OPV, and Hepatitis B vaccinations for newborns immediately after birth.

WATERBORNE PATHOGENS AND SANITATION:
- Outbreaks of gastroenteritis, hepatitis A, and typhoid spike during monsoon seasons due to water contamination.
- Public health instruction: Consume only boiled or chlorine-treated drinking water.
- Proper food safety practices: 'Cook it, peel it, boil it, or forget it'. Handwashing before meals and after toilet use reduces disease transmission by 40%.
