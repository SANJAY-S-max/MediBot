1. ABSTRACT
================================================================================
MediBot AI is a production-ready, full-stack AI-powered public healthcare
management platform designed to bridge the critical gap in healthcare access for
India's rural and underserved populations.

The system integrates clinical triage intelligence, capability-aware hospital
routing (Haversine geodesic ranking), role-based dashboards for all four
principal stakeholders — Patient, ASHA/AHA Worker, Doctor, and Admin — with a
25+ entity Neon PostgreSQL database, LangGraph multi-agent RAG knowledge engine,
Jitsi WebRTC telemedicine, and 108/102 ambulance dispatch integration.

It classifies patient presentations into P1 Emergency / P2 Urgent / P3 Routine
using physiological parameters (SpO₂, Heart Rate, Systolic BP, Respiratory
Rate, Consciousness/AVPU) and symptom pattern matching, then routes each case
to the nearest verified, capability-matched public healthcare facility across
the full facility tier hierarchy (Sub-Centre → PHC → CHC → SDH → District
Hospital → Medical College).

================================================================================
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
India's rural healthcare system faces three core structural challenges:

  (a) INFORMATION GAP:  Patients in rural areas are unaware of nearby public
      health facilities, their available services, bed availability, and
      appropriate referral pathways.

  (b) TRIAGE GAP:  Without trained personnel, families misidentify severity
      and either under-utilize PHCs (travelling far for minor ailments) or
      catastrophically delay emergency referrals to District Hospitals.

  (c) CONTINUITY GAP:  ASHA/AHA field workers perform periodic community
      health checkups but lack a structured digital system for vitals logging,
      medication compliance monitoring, and escalation to doctors.

MediBot AI solves all three through a single, integrated platform.

3. OBJECTIVES
================================================================================
  1. Clinical Triage Automation
     → AI-based P1/P2/P3 triage using SpO₂, BP, Heart Rate, RR, AVPU,
       and symptom pattern recognition per Indian Emergency Medicine guidelines.

  2. Capability-Aware Hospital Recommendation
     → Geodesic Haversine routing to nearest verified public facility whose
       equipment and specialty capacity matches the clinical requirement.

  3. Emergency Dispatch Integration
     → Automated 108 ALS / 102 Maternal BLS ambulance payload with GPS
       coordinates, patient ABHA ID, triage summary, and digital QR referral slip.

  4. AHA/ASHA Worker Community Health Monitoring
     → Structured 7-step checkup checklists for community field visits,
       automated doctor escalation for abnormal vitals or medication non-compliance.

  5. Doctor Clinical Queue & Telemedicine
     → Live triage queue, AHA field escalation review, prescription issuance,
       and WebRTC (Jitsi) telemedicine consultations.

  6. Admin System Governance
     → Real-time Neon DB health dashboards, hospital registry management,
       user RBAC management, checklist template builder, and immutable audit log.

  7. RAG Knowledge Engine
     → LangGraph + LangChain hybrid retrieval (ChromaDB dense + BM25 sparse +
       Cross-Encoder re-ranking) grounded by Groq LLaMA 3 70B generation
       with clinical knowledge base citations.

  8. Multilingual AI Assistant
     → Voice-enabled symptom checker in English, Hindi, and Tamil via
       Google Gemini and Groq API.

5. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & USER FLOWS
================================================================================

  ROLE 1 — PATIENT  (Route: /dashboard)
  ──────────────────────────────────────
  • View personal medical history, diagnoses, prescriptions, lab reports
  • View active care plan and upcoming AHA checkup schedule
  • Search nearby hospitals by capability
  • Trigger "I'm in Trouble" → guided triage intake modal:
      Step 1: Symptom selection (multi-select grid)
      Step 2: Severity & duration input
      Step 3: Vital signs entry (SpO₂, HR, BP, RR, Temp, Consciousness)
      Step 4: Live AI triage result (P1/P2/P3 with clinical reasoning)
      Step 5: Ranked hospital recommendations with distance & capabilities
      Step 6: 108/102 ambulance dispatch + SVG QR referral slip download

  ROLE 2 — AHA WORKER  (Route: /aha)
  ────────────────────────────────────
  • View assigned village patients with chronic condition summaries & ABHA IDs
  • See due/overdue checkup visit counts and urgency badges
  • Execute configurable 7-step checkup checklist modal:
      Step 1: Visit verification & patient identification (ABHA/Aadhaar scan)
      Step 2: Vitals measurement (SpO₂, BP, Heart Rate, Blood Glucose, Temp)
      Step 3: Medication adherence assessment
      Step 4: Previous follow-up review
      Step 5: Active symptom reporting (freetext + multi-select)
      Step 6: Clinical field observations
      Step 7: Doctor escalation decision (reason + priority level)
  • Checkup data written to Neon DB in real-time with full audit trail

  ROLE 3 — DOCTOR  (Route: /doctor)
  ────────────────────────────────────
  • Real-time triage case queue (P1 critical cases highlighted in red)
  • AHA field escalation review list with field worker observations
  • Clinical Review Modal:
      → Review AHA checkup record and vitals trend
      → Record doctor review notes & confirmed diagnosis
      → Issue new prescription (medication, dosage, frequency, duration)
  • Live hospital bed capacity tracker (ICU / O₂ beds / Ventilator beds)
  • Launch Jitsi WebRTC telemedicine consultation session

  ROLE 4 — ADMIN  (Route: /admin)
  ────────────────────────────────────
  • Live System Health Dashboard:
      → Neon DB connection latency (ms)
      → Auth service status
      → Triage engine status
      → Map & recommendation engine status
  • Hospital Infrastructure Registry:
      → All verified public hospitals (Sub-Centres to Medical Colleges)
      → Bed inventory management (total / available / ICU / O₂ / Ventilator)
  • User & Role Management table (create, view, deactivate accounts)
  • Configurable Checklist Template Builder (add/edit/delete checklist steps)
  • Immutable Security Audit Log stream (all sensitive actions logged)

8. CLINICAL TRIAGE ENGINE — DECISION RULES
================================================================================

  File: frontend/lib/recommendationEngine.ts

  PHYSIOLOGICAL P1 EMERGENCY TRIGGERS:
  ┌──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Parameter                │ P1 Threshold                                  │
  ├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ SpO₂ Oxygen Saturation   │ < 90%  → Critical Hypoxemia                  │
  │ Heart Rate               │ > 135 bpm OR < 45 bpm → Severe Arrhythmia    │
  │ Systolic Blood Pressure  │ ≥ 190 mmHg OR < 90 mmHg → HTN Crisis / Shock │
  │ Respiratory Rate         │ > 30 OR < 8 breaths/min → Impending RF       │
  │ Consciousness (AVPU)     │ "Pain" or "Unresponsive" → Coma / Seizure     │
  └──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  SYMPTOM PATTERN P1 TRIGGERS:
  • Chest pain / heart attack / palpitation → Suspected ACS / STEMI
  • Head injury + severe trauma → 24x7 Trauma Unit required
  • Maternal bleeding + obstetric complications → High-risk OT + NICU
  • Snakebite / poisoning / envenomation → Anti-Snake Venom + Ventilator

  P2 URGENT TRIGGERS:
  • SpO₂ 91–94% → Supplementary oxygen (CHC level)
  • Systolic BP 150–189 mmHg → ECG + specialist monitoring
  • Pregnancy / labor without complications → Maternity Ward + Sonography
  • Fracture / trauma (non-head) → X-Ray + Ortho OT

  P3 ROUTINE:
  • All normal vital signs, mild fever, cold, routine infections
  • → Nearest PHC / Sub-Centre referral

  HOSPITAL MATCHING SCORE (100-point ranking):
  • 60% — Capability match rate (required capabilities vs. hospital equipment)
  • 25% — Distance proximity score (Haversine distance, max ~40 km)
  • 15% — Bed availability ratio (available/total × 100)

  ┌────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

  ├────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

  │ RerankingNode      │ Cross-Encoder (ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2) re-ranking     │
  │ GenerationNode     │ Groq LLaMA 3 70B grounded answer generation           │
  │ FacilityNode       │ Haversine distance + capability matrix matching       │
  │ EscalationNode     │ Generate referral pathway + 108/102 dispatch payload  │
  └────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  RAG RETRIEVAL PIPELINE:
  Query → [Dense: ChromaDB + HuggingFace Embeddings]
        → [Sparse: BM25 Retriever (TF-IDF keyword)]
        → [Re-Rank: Cross-Encoder (top-K merging)]
        → [Generate: Groq LLaMA 3 70B with retrieved context]
        → Grounded answer with document citation sources

  • Indian Public Health System clinical manuals
  • AYUSH + Ayushman Bharat scheme documents
  • NHM (National Health Mission) facility capability guides
  • IDSP (Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme) protocols
  • Facility-level equipment inventories (ingest_facilities.py)

16. KEY INNOVATIONS & DIFFERENTIATORS
================================================================================

  1. REAL-TIME CLINICAL TRIAGE ENGINE
     Not just keyword matching — uses actual clinical parameters (SpO₂, HR, BP,
     RR, AVPU) aligned with Indian Emergency Medicine and NHM triage protocols
     to produce evidence-based P1/P2/P3 classification.

  2. CAPABILITY-AWARE GEODESIC HOSPITAL ROUTER
     Haversine great-circle distance combined with equipment capability matrix
     scoring (60% capability + 25% proximity + 15% bed availability) ensures
     patients are never sent to a hospital that lacks the required equipment.

  3. DIGITAL SVG QR REFERRAL SLIP
     Auto-generated referral slip containing patient ABHA ID, triage summary,
     required capabilities, and recommended hospital — scannable at facility
     reception for instant triage queue entry.

  4. AHA WORKER DIGITAL CHECKUP SYSTEM
     Replaces paper-based ASHA registers with structured digital checklists,
     real-time vitals capture, and automated doctor escalation — creating a
     continuous community health monitoring loop.

  5. HYBRID RAG KNOWLEDGE ENGINE
     Combines dense ChromaDB semantic search + BM25 keyword search + Cross-
     Encoder re-ranking for superior retrieval accuracy over either approach
     alone — grounded by Groq LLaMA 3 70B generation with citations.

  6. FULLY SERVERLESS DATABASE
     Neon PostgreSQL serverless eliminates database hosting costs while
     providing automatic scaling for variable healthcare query loads.

  7. IMMUTABLE AUDIT TRAIL
     Every sensitive medical action (prescription, diagnosis, escalation,
     admin role change) is written to an append-only AuditLog table in
     Neon PostgreSQL — ensuring regulatory compliance and accountability.
